# Week 1 — Cybersecurity Fundamentals

This week focused on building a basic understanding of cybersecurity concepts and how common cyber threats work.

## Topics Covered

- CIA Triad
- Authentication, Authorization & Accounting (AAA)
- Types of Hackers
- Cyber Attack Lifecycle
- Malware
- Social Engineering

## What I Learned

### 1. CIA Triad
Learned the three core principles of cybersecurity:
- Confidentiality
- Integrity
- Availability

### 2. AAA
Learned the fundamentals of:
- Authentication
- Authorization
- Accounting

### 3. Types of Hackers
Studied different types of hackers and their purposes, including:
- White Hat
- Black Hat
- Grey Hat

### 4. Cyber Attack Lifecycle
Learned the general stages involved in a cyber attack, from reconnaissance to achieving the attacker's objective.

### 5. Malware
Studied common types of malicious software:
- Virus
- Worm
- Trojan
- Ransomware
- Spyware

### 6. Social Engineering
Learned how attackers manipulate people rather than directly attacking technical systems.

Covered examples such as:
- Phishing
- Pretexting
- Baiting
- Tailgating

## Practical Learning

- Studied real-world examples of cyber attacks.
- Practiced identifying common social engineering techniques.
- Created notes and diagrams for the concepts covered.
