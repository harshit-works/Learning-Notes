# Types of Hackers

Hackers can be categorized based on their intentions, authorization, and activities.

## 1. White Hat Hacker

White hat hackers are security professionals who perform authorized security testing.

Their goal is to identify vulnerabilities and help organizations improve their security.

**Example:** An organization hires a penetration tester to find vulnerabilities in its systems.

---

## 2. Black Hat Hacker

Black hat hackers access systems or data without authorization, usually for malicious or illegal purposes.

Their activities may include:

- Data theft
- Financial fraud
- Malware distribution
- Unauthorized system access

---

## 3. Grey Hat Hacker

Grey hat hackers operate between the traditional white-hat and black-hat categories.

They may discover or test vulnerabilities without explicit authorization but may not have malicious intentions.

Unauthorized testing can still have legal and ethical consequences.

---

## 4. Script Kiddie

A script kiddie is someone who uses existing hacking tools or scripts without necessarily understanding how they work internally.

They generally have limited technical knowledge compared with experienced security professionals.

---

## 5. Hacktivist

Hacktivists use hacking techniques to promote a political, social, or ideological cause.

Their activities may include website disruption, information leaks, or other forms of cyber activism.

---

## 6. Insider Threat

An insider threat comes from someone who already has legitimate access to an organization's systems or information.

Examples include:

- A malicious employee
- A compromised employee account
- Accidental data exposure by an employee
