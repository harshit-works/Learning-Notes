# Cyber Attack Lifecycle

The cyber attack lifecycle describes the general stages an attacker may go through when attempting to compromise a target.

Different security frameworks use different names and numbers of stages.

## 1. Reconnaissance

The attacker gathers information about the target.

Examples of information may include:

- Domain names
- IP addresses
- Publicly available information
- Technologies being used

---

## 2. Weaponization

The attacker prepares the tools, files, or techniques that may be used during an attack.

---

## 3. Delivery

The attack method or malicious content is delivered to the target.

Examples can include:

- Malicious email attachments
- Phishing messages
- Compromised websites

---

## 4. Exploitation

The attacker attempts to take advantage of a vulnerability or weakness.

The weakness could exist in:

- Software
- Applications
- Configurations
- Human behavior

---

## 5. Installation

If the attack succeeds, malicious software or another mechanism may be installed to maintain access.

---

## 6. Command and Control

A compromised system may communicate with infrastructure controlled by the attacker.

This can allow the attacker to send instructions to the compromised system.

---

## 7. Actions on Objectives

The attacker performs the final objective of the attack.

Examples include:

- Stealing data
- Disrupting services
- Encrypting files
- Modifying information
